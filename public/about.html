Our Story

In the highlands, many children face nutritional gaps despite the best efforts of their families and teachers. Therefore, we strive to combine growth measurements, WHO growth standards and locally available ingredients to recommend meals suited to children in each nutritional group, then connect donors so those meals can reach the children.

Measure & group

Regular height and weight checks; children grouped using WHO growth standards.

Menu recommendation model

Proposes meals per group, balancing available ingredients and budget.

Donate & track

Each contribution is linked to specific meals and updated publicly.

Sincere thanks to Ước Mơ Vùng Biên(Borderland Dreams) Organization for walking this journey with us.
